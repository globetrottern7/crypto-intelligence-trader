# Product Requirements — P0 v1.0

## Objective
Personal, learning-first digital-asset market intelligence and paper-trading system. Prove data quality, strategy validity, risk controls and after-cost accounting before considering live execution.

## Access
Desktop website, responsive mobile website, optional installable Android PWA. Shared backend, identity, settings and portfolio.

## Investment universe
- Intraday: 30–50 liquid cryptocurrencies selected by documented liquidity and data-quality rules.
- Swing: broader digital-asset universe including major cryptocurrencies, selected altcoins, screened emerging assets and eligible tokenised assets.
- Stablecoins: primarily liquidity and reserve instruments, with depeg, issuer, custody and liquidity monitoring.

## P0 modules
Market data; dashboard/scanner; historical intelligence; intraday engine; swing engine; Risk Guardian; Strategy Lab; paper trading; performance/journal; dynamic capital allocation; AI Mentor.

## Exclusions
No live exchange order execution, futures, leverage, copy trading, commercial subscriptions or unrestricted autonomous AI trading.

## Principles
No forced trades or fabricated/stale data. Quantitative rules and risk checks are deterministic. AI explains but cannot bypass controls. Backtests avoid look-ahead bias and include realistic costs. Paper trading models fees, spread, slippage and execution limitations. Tax records remain transaction-level; daily net P&L is not a tax calculation.
